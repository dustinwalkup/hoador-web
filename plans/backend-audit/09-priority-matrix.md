# Priority matrix

All 117 consolidated findings, ranked. Totals: CRITICAL 1 · HIGH 24 (17 defects + 7 test gaps that sit behind them) · MEDIUM 58 · LOW 34.

**How the ranking works.** Priority (P0–P3) was assigned per finding using the order of concerns requested for this audit: security impact, then financial impact, privacy impact, data integrity, cross-user impact, exploitability, blast radius, production reliability, and finally fix complexity. Within a priority band, rows are ordered by severity, then by the most important impact dimension (Security > Financial > Privacy > Integrity > Reliability), then by fix size (small first).

- **P0** – fix before (or immediately after) the next production release; exploitable today or silently losing money/data.
- **P1** – fix within the next 1–2 sprints.
- **P2** – schedule within the quarter.
- **P3** – hardening / opportunistic.
- **Phase** refers to `10-remediation-roadmap.md` (0 = hotfix window, 1 = stabilization, 2 = hardening, 3 = structural/opportunistic; ranges like `1-3` span phases).
- **Fix**: S ≈ under a day, M ≈ 1–3 days, L ≈ a week or more (including tests).
- **Who can trigger** describes the weakest attacker or the trigger condition; "Passive" means it happens in normal operation without anyone acting maliciously.
